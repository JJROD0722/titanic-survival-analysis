Titanic Survival Analysis

I used Altair AI Studio (RapidMiner) to explore 891 Titanic passenger records and compare survival across passenger classes.

What I did

Summarized passenger data with descriptive statistics, frequency counts, and a Pearson correlation matrix.

One-hot encoded passenger class, sex, and embarkation port.

Created charts comparing fare with age, survival counts by class, and average age by survival status.

What I found

About 63% of first-class passengers survived, compared with 47% in second class and 24% in third class. Among passengers with a recorded age, survivors averaged 28.34 years and non-survivors averaged 30.63 years.

The report includes screenshots of the RapidMiner workflow and results, plus a proposal for testing survival prediction models.
